What we deliver

Three concrete outputs, in this order. Each one is useful on its own — you don't need to buy the whole pipeline to get value from the first step.

service/01

Data quality analysis

A structured audit of what your data actually contains, versus what your systems assume it contains.

  • Completeness & consistency scoring
  • Schema and lineage mapping
  • Bias and drift checks
  • Fit-for-purpose report, ranked by risk
service/02

AI potential assessment

Where AI genuinely changes an outcome for you, and where it's a distraction dressed up as innovation.

  • Use-case shortlist, scored on impact & feasibility
  • Build vs. buy vs. skip recommendation
  • Cost and risk envelope per use case
  • Plain-language technical rationale
service/03

Implementation plans

A sequenced, staffed, and testable path from the assessment to something running in production.

  • Architecture & system design
  • Milestones with acceptance criteria
  • Evaluation & monitoring plan
  • Handover documentation for your team

Founder & principal consultant background: mathematics + computer science

Euclidean Analytics is built around a single practitioner's background in mathematics and computer science, not a generic delivery team. That means engagements start from first principles — what the data can actually support, what a model can actually estimate, what error rates a decision can actually tolerate — rather than from a template slide deck.

Before founding Euclidean Analytics, this meant building probabilistic models, computer vision pipelines, and applied ML systems where the cost of an overconfident answer was measured in real operational failures, not just a lower accuracy score. That discipline carries directly into how every audit, assessment, and roadmap here is written: specific claims, stated assumptions, and numbers that are defensible under questioning.

How an engagement runs

Five stages, always in this order — each one produces something you keep, even if you stop after that stage.

01

Assess

Audit the data you have: completeness, consistency, lineage, and known failure modes. Output: a written data quality report.

02

Prioritize

Map candidate AI use cases against feasibility, expected impact, and the quality gaps found in step one. Output: a ranked shortlist.

03

Plan

Turn the top use case(s) into an implementation plan with architecture, milestones, and acceptance criteria. Output: a roadmap document.

04

Build & deploy

Implement the system — alone or alongside your engineers — with evaluation baked in from the first commit, not bolted on at the end.

05

Operate & iterate

Monitor real-world performance against the plan's acceptance criteria and adjust. Output: a monitoring dashboard and revision log.
