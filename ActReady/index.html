    From a plain description to a compliance roadmap

    1. DescribeExplain the use case in your own words.

    3. AssessAn indicative risk category and a readiness indicator per use case.
    4. Close gapsA checklist of the open gaps.
    5. MonitorRe-check the whole portfolio when a new law or amendment passes.

    A use case portfolio is the foundation of compliance

    Without a living portfolio, there is no way to know, or prove, where you stand.

    Obligations depend on role

    Provider, deployer, importer or distributor: duties differ, and one company often holds several roles across its use cases.

    Classification is per use case

    Risk class follows purpose and context, so it cannot be assessed once for the whole company.

    Governance needs an inventory

    Owners, documentation, oversight and an audit trail attach to each use case. They are the evidence regulators and customers ask for.

    When the law changes, you see what it means for you

    ActReady watches new bills and amendments, matches them to your portfolio and re-checks only the use cases they affect.

    1Alert

    Amendment adopted
    Changes obligations for high-risk systems used in employment and credit.

    Sent to the portfolio owner and the owners of affected use cases.

    2Impact

    • CV screening (deployer)
      New logging requirement applies.
    • Credit scoring (provider)
      Deadline for documentation changes.
    • Support chatbot and demand forecasting: not affected.

    CV screening72%→ 64%
    Credit scoring58%→ 58%

    Planned: your use cases stay yours, even from us

    Use case descriptions often describe your product ideas and strategy. We are designing ActReady so that this intellectual property is protected by design.

    Encrypted, end to end

    Use case descriptions and the portfolio will be encrypted with a key that only the account owner holds. We will store ciphertext only, so our staff, and anyone with access to our systems, cannot read it.

    Plain text for the owner only

    Only the account owner will be able to reveal the plain text version. Sharing with colleagues or advisors will be a deliberate choice you make, never a default.

    A one-way analysis engine

    The text analysis is designed to work in one direction: a description goes in, and only an indicative risk category, a readiness indicator and a gap list come out. It is designed not to keep, log or return the underlying text.
