    A use case portfolio is the foundation of compliance

    Without a living portfolio, there is no way to know, or prove, where you stand.

    Obligations depend on role

    Provider, deployer, importer or distributor: duties differ, and one company often holds several roles across its use cases.

    Classification is per use case

    Risk class follows purpose and context, so it cannot be assessed once for the whole company.

    Governance needs an inventory

    Owners, documentation, oversight and an audit trail attach to each use case. They are the evidence regulators and customers ask for.

    Your use cases stay yours, even from us

    Use case descriptions often describe your product ideas and strategy. We built ActReady so that this intellectual property is protected by design.

    Encrypted, end to end

    Your use case descriptions and your portfolio are encrypted with a key that only the account owner holds. We store ciphertext only, so our staff, and anyone with access to our systems, cannot read it.

    Plain text for the owner only

    Only the account owner can reveal the plain text version. Sharing with colleagues or advisors is a deliberate choice you make, never a default.

    A one-way analysis engine

    Our text analysis works in one direction: a description goes in, and only a risk class, a score and a gap list come out. It does not keep, log or return the underlying text, so nothing about your use cases leaks.
